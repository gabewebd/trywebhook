ABOUT THE ROLE

Façade & Architectural Skilled Drafter

We are looking for motivated, agile team players who are excited to build new capabilities and keep growing. As part of our small but talented team, you will work remotely alongside our façade engineering director, providing consulting services on new and existing façade projects, both commercial and residential.

Key responsibilities

  • Draft detailed designs and documentation for façade systems, including glass façades and various cladding types, in line with project requirements and industry standards
  • Review drawings and maintain document control throughout the drafting process, ensuring accuracy and compliance with specifications
  • Design façades for high-rise buildings, balancing structural integrity, aesthetic appeal and functional requirements
  • Provide specialist input to resolve technical issues and uphold the quality of the team's products and services
  • Review shop drawings and material/method statements submitted by contractors, ensuring alignment with project specifications
  • Use AutoCAD and Revit proficiently to produce accurate, detailed drawings
  • Apply knowledge of building design concepts, construction practices and relevant Australian Standards to every drafting task
  • Collaborate with project managers, builders and architects to keep communication clear and façade projects on track

Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in Architecture, Engineering or a related field
  • 5+ years of hands-on experience as a façade drafter, with expertise in Capral/Alspec aluminium window systems, overseas window wall systems and cladding systems (e.g. CSR, James Hardie, Equitone, Fairview)
  • Proficient in reviewing drawings and maintaining document control processes
  • Experience drafting façades for high-rise buildings, with a solid understanding of structural considerations and building regulations
  • Expert-level AutoCAD skills, producing accurate, well-organised and fully coordinated drawings with minimal supervision
  • Working knowledge of Autodesk Revit and its fundamental tools
  • Experience transitioning from AutoCAD to Revit is an advantage
  • Thorough knowledge of the National Construction Code (NCC) and Australian Standards for façade design and construction
  • Strong problem-solving skills and the ability to perform under pressure to meet deadlines
  • Excellent communication skills and leadership potential, working effectively with project stakeholders
  • Passion for continued personal development in structural and/or façade drafting
  • Experience in detailed façade shop drawings and liaising with project managers, builders and architects

How success is measured (KPIs)

  • Timely completion of drafting tasks, meeting project deadlines and milestones
  • Accuracy and precision of drafted designs, minimising errors and revisions
  • Compliance with the National Construction Code and Australian Standards in all documentation
  • Effectiveness in resolving technical problems and improving project outcomes through specialist input
  • Quality of reviewed shop drawings and material/method statements
  • Efficient, high-quality drawings that show strong CAD proficiency
  • Smooth coordination and communication with project stakeholders
  • Contribution to the growth of the drafting team, including sharing expertise and mentoring junior drafters
