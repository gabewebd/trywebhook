ABOUT THE ROLE

Accounts & Administration Officer

We are seeking a highly organized, detail-oriented and proactive Accounts & Administration Officer to support the Managing Director and contribute to the smooth operation of the business. This role combines executive and administrative assistance with accounts payable, accounts receivable, payroll administration, appointment scheduling and client coordination. The ideal candidate works independently on routine tasks while communicating effectively with management, clients and finance personnel. Previous experience supporting Australian businesses is an advantage but not mandatory.

Key responsibilities

  • Executive & administrative support: assist the Managing Director, manage calendars and meetings, organize correspondence and documents, prepare agendas and minutes, and track deadlines and follow-ups
  • Accounts payable & receivable: process supplier invoices and payments, prepare customer invoices, record transactions, follow up overdue invoices, and assist with bank reconciliations
  • Payroll administration: gather and review payroll information, maintain payroll records, monitor timesheets and leave, prepare or coordinate payroll, and flag discrepancies
  • Calendar & office coordination: schedule meetings, manage confirmations and reminders, maintain digital filing systems, and suggest improvements to admin workflows
  • Client communication: respond to routine client emails and inquiries, coordinate client meetings and documents, provide timely updates, and escalate complex matters to the Managing Director
  • Handle confidential business, financial and employee information with strict confidentiality

Qualifications

  • Around 3–5 years of experience in accounts administration, office administration, executive assistance, bookkeeping support or payroll administration
  • Degree in Business Administration, Accounting, Finance or a related discipline
  • Hands-on experience with accounts payable and/or accounts receivable
  • Practical experience in payroll processing or payroll administration
  • Experience managing schedules, business correspondence and documentation
  • Strong Microsoft Excel, Word and Outlook skills
  • Good numerical accuracy and attention to financial records
  • Strong written and verbal English communication skills
  • Able to manage competing priorities and meet deadlines with minimal supervision
  • Professional judgment and commitment to confidentiality
  • Experience directly supporting a Managing Director, business owner or senior executive
  • Familiarity with Xero, MYOB, QuickBooks or comparable accounting systems
  • Experience with bank reconciliations, payroll reports and bookkeeping tasks
  • Familiarity with Australian payroll practices (an advantage)
  • Experience in a small or medium-sized business with varied admin responsibilities
