ABOUT THE ROLE

What you'll be doing

We're looking for a hands-on Senior IT Support specialist to keep our people, offices and client sites running smoothly. You'll own network setup and troubleshooting, both onsite and remotely, and be the go-to person who solves technical issues quickly, explains them clearly, and helps us keep improving how we work.

Key responsibilities

  • Set up, configure and maintain wired and wireless networks, including routers, switches, access points and internet connections
  • Diagnose and resolve connectivity, hardware and software issues, both onsite and remotely
  • Provide responsive technical support to staff and, when needed, to client sites
  • Set up and maintain workstations, laptops, peripherals and user accounts
  • Coordinate with internet service providers and IT vendors on installations, outages and repairs
  • Keep IT documentation, network diagrams and equipment inventory up to date
  • Support new office and site setups, including travel for onsite deployments
  • Recommend practical improvements to keep our systems reliable, secure and efficient

What we're looking for

  • Currently or recently employed in an IT support or network role
  • At least 2 years of IT support experience in the same or a similar industry
  • Strong track record setting up and troubleshooting wired and wireless networks, both onsite and remotely
  • Sharp critical thinking and a logical, step-by-step approach to problem solving
  • Open to coaching, and sees constructive feedback as a chance to grow
  • Acts on feedback quickly and keeps improving how you work
  • Comfortable giving honest, respectful feedback and sharing ideas openly
  • Thrives with a leader who sets high standards and challenges the team to do their best
  • Stays calm and delivers under tight deadlines and short-notice requests
  • Open to flexible yet reasonable working hours, based on business needs
  • Willing to travel for onsite support and deployments
