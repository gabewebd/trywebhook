ABOUT THE ROLE

Microsoft 365 / Teams Specialist (Project-Based)

We're looking for an experienced IT specialist to help our Philippine team communicate reliably with our Singapore-based client. This is a project-based engagement with an urgent start.

Must-have skills

Candidates are required to have hands-on experience in the following. You will be asked about each one in the application form.

MICROSOFT TEAMSExternal & guest accessFederation, external messaging and guest (B2B) setup
MICROSOFT 365Tenant & domain administrationAccounts, domains, licensing and admin settings
NETWORKNetwork & internet diagnosticsOr able to bring in a network specialist
FILE ACCESSFile servers & mapped drivesNAS, shared drives and VPN access
APPLICATIONSApp sign-in & firewall troubleshootinge.g. AutoCAD / Autodesk authentication
WAY OF WORKINGSafe change managementApprovals, rollback plans and least-privilege access
COORDINATIONVendor & support liaisonMicrosoft support, internet providers and client IT
DOCUMENTATIONClear findings & handoverWritten reports and support instructions

What you'll do

  • Troubleshoot Microsoft Teams access and messaging issues
  • Check our Microsoft 365 account setup
  • Determine whether network or internet issues are contributing to the problem
  • Identify what's wrong and implement a safe fix
  • Leave us with a stable setup and clear support instructions

What we're looking for

  • Experienced IT specialist with a strong Microsoft 365 and Teams background
  • Hands-on experience with Teams external access and guest access
  • Microsoft 365 tenant, domain and account administration
  • Able to diagnose network and internet issues, or bring in a network specialist when needed
  • Makes changes safely, with approvals, rollback plans and limited access
  • Available for an urgent start, in Philippine / Singapore time (GMT+8)
  • Comfortable providing remote support; onsite availability in Angeles City, Pampanga, is an advantage
