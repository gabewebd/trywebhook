ABOUT THE ROLE

Mechanical & Electrical BIM Modeler

The BIM Modeler is responsible for providing support in various BIM modeling tasks, from building 3D models in Revit to producing accurate, well-documented shop drawings and coordinating with other disciplines.

Key responsibilities

  • Build 3D models and prepare drawings using Autodesk Revit
  • Update the 3D model with the latest information to keep shop drawings accurate
  • Collaborate and coordinate with other disciplines on design changes and model challenges
  • Prepare BIM models that support visualization, communication and informed decisions on design changes
  • Ensure M&E designs comply with relevant codes and regulations
  • Produce M&E Combined Services Drawings (CSD), builders' work drawings and detailed shop drawings following design criteria and specifications
  • Perform clash detection
  • Adhere to the BIM Execution Plan and BIM project workflow criteria
  • Follow BIM content standards and development procedures
  • Ensure proper documentation of all drawings and maintain modeling quality control
  • Perform other duties as assigned

Qualifications

  • Degree in MEP or equivalent
  • Minimum 2–3 years of relevant working experience
  • Proficiency in Autodesk Revit is a must
  • AutoCAD skills for drawing linkage and cleanup
  • Knowledge of BIM requirements under Singapore standards is a plus
  • A team player with a sense of responsibility
